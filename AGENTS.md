# AGENTS.md — PERMANENT RULES FOR YOUTUBE SHORTS PUBLISHING

Har ek subagent aur script ke liye mandatory rules:

1. **Zero Mismatched Audio**: Alag se robotic/mismatched voiceover add nahi karna. Hamesha AI video ki 100% original voice rahegi.
2. **Dual-Audio Mandatory**: Viral trending sound (Monkeys Spinning Monkeys) at 0% volume (`volume=0.0`) attach hona chahiye.
3. **Bilingual Title**: Hindi + English + Emojis + #Shorts.
4. **Description**: Story + 24/7 Live Stream Link + Viral Hashtags.
5. **Tags**: 500-characters of rich keywords.
6. **Related Video**: YouTube Studio me Related Video link select karna zaroori hai.
7. **Language**: Video Language = Hindi.
8. **Thumbnail**: Click-based 3D Thumbnail (Top Yellow / Bottom White stroked text).
9. **Zero-Draft Policy**: Koi bhi upload Draft me nahi chhodna; complete karke schedule karna hai.
