# 📈 YouTube SEO Optimizer Skill

This skill provides data-driven algorithms and runbooks for optimizing YouTube Shorts and Videos to achieve maximum discovery in **YouTube Shorts Feed**, **Search**, and **Suggested Videos**.

---

## 🎯 1. High-CTR Title Architecture (Bilingual & Hook)
For toddler & 3D animation channels, titles must satisfy both **Emotional Curiosity** and **Search Intent**:
- **Format**: `[Hindi Devanagari Hook] [Emoji] [English Search Title] #Shorts`
- **Example**: `मम्मी की लिपस्टिक और बिंदी! 💄🤣 Kaavya Makeup Fun #Shorts`
- **Rules**:
  1. Character limit: Under 70 characters so title never truncates on mobile.
  2. First 3 words must contain the emotional punch (`मम्मी की लिपस्टिक`, `चूहे को लगा बुखार`).
  3. Include 1–2 highly expressive emojis (🤣, 💄, 🐱, 🍼).
  4. Always end with `#Shorts`.

---

## 🏷️ 2. The 500-Character Tag Pyramid
Never use random tags. Fill tags to 450–495 characters using the 4-tier tag pyramid:
1. **Tier 1 - Brand Anchor (15%)**: `The Naughty Duo`, `TheNaughtyDuo`, `Naughty Duo`
2. **Tier 2 - Exact Topic & Episode (35%)**: `Mummy Ki Lipstick`, `Lipstick Comedy`, `Kaavya Makeup`, `Kaavya and Kaartik`
3. **Tier 3 - Category Keywords (30%)**: `Hindi Rhymes`, `Kids Cartoon Hindi`, `Toddler Stories`, `3D Animation Hindi`, `Moral Stories For Kids`
4. **Tier 4 - High-Search Broader Intent (20%)**: `Cartoon for toddlers`, `Funny baby videos`, `Chuchu tv style`, `Cocomelon Hindi`

---

## 📝 3. Rich Description & Retention Loop
YouTube uses the first 3 lines of description for indexing.
- **Line 1**: Episode synopsis with keywords naturally woven in.
- **Line 2**: Direct link to 24/7 Channel Live Stream (`https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCULzzCCm0Y-ZHiyF480ioLg/live`).
- **Line 3**: Call to Action ("Subscribe for daily 3D cartoon adventures!").
- **Hashtags block**: `#TheNaughtyDuo #Shorts #HindiCartoons #Animation #KidsStories`

---

## ⚡ 4. Retention & Pacing Rules (Shorts Feed Algorithm)
- **First 1.5 Seconds (Hook)**: Immediate visual conflict or laughter. Never show a logo or slow fade-in.
- **Pacing**: Cut or motion change every 1.8 to 2.5 seconds to maintain Average Percentage Viewed (APV) > 85%.
- **Audio Hook**: Original clip voices loud and crisp (`volume=1.0`), trending audio tied at `volume=0.0` for feed indexing.
- **Loop Seamlessness**: The end frame should naturally flow into the first frame for replay loops.
